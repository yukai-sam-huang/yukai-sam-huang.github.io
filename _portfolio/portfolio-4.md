You can download the paper here: [arXiv:2606.15097](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.15097)

**Abstract**: We study how winsorizing extreme outcomes affects difference-in-differences estimates in a two-group, two-period setting. For continuous outcomes with finite variance, winsorizing separately within each group-period cell provides a uniform sub-Gaussian deviation guarantee when the winsorization fraction is calibrated. Raw and pooled estimators cannot achieve the same guarantee. We identify conditions for asymptotic unbiasedness under separate winsorization and show that pooled winsorization may remain biased. We also identify a two-sided decreasing-failure-rate condition under which separate winsorization reduces asymptotic variance relative to the raw estimator.
