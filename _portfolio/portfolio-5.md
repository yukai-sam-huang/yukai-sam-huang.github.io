**Abstract**: We study joint inventory and assortment optimization under general substitutable choice. Before customers arrive, a decision maker allocates limited inventory. During the selling horizon, the decision maker either offers all in-stock products or uses the customer's type to personalize the assortment. We connect both settings to static assortment problems and derive approximation guarantees for deterministic, Poisson, and increasing-failure-rate arrivals. The personalized policy fixes an assortment for each customer type before the selling horizon and offers the products in that assortment that remain in stock.
